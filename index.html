01 · Evidence

Three complementary signals

The strongest result is convergence: genes that respond to salinity and lie near independently published salinity GWAS loci receive higher priority.

01

Transcriptomic response

Expression changes provide the biological response layer. Candidates are ranked by effect size and expression evidence.

02

Published GWAS support

A ±100 kb window links ranked expression candidates to published salinity-associated SNP coordinates.

03

Genomic prediction

CatBoost and other models provide a secondary SNP-neighborhood signal under population-blocked validation.

02 · Models

Predictive performance is reported honestly

The models are useful as prioritization support, not as a finished field-ready predictor. The population-blocked CatBoost model reached ROC-AUC 0.584.

Method note

Designed for transparent prioritization

Objective 1 ranks salinity-responsive expression features. Objective 2 integrates published salinity GWAS coordinates with a ±100 kb window. Objective 3 evaluates baseline, regularized logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost models using population-blocked validation where possible.
